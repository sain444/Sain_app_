# Privacy Policy for Sainn

**Last updated:** [FILL IN DATE WHEN YOU PUBLISH THIS]

Sainn ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated by Sainn (contact: tutiongpt@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

## Information we collect

**Account information**
- Phone number (used to create your account and verify your identity via a one-time code)
- Display name and profile photo, if you choose to add them
- Username, if you set one

**Content you send**
- Messages, photos, videos, files, and voice messages you send through the App
- Stories/updates you post (automatically deleted after 24 hours)

**Usage and technical information**
- Online/last-seen status and typing indicators
- Message delivery and read receipts (unless you disable read receipts in Settings)
- Call metadata: who called whom, call duration, and call type (audio/video) — **not** the content of calls, which is transmitted directly between callers and is not stored by us
- Device push notification token, used solely to deliver notifications to your device
- IP address and basic device information, collected automatically by our servers for security and abuse prevention

**Information about other people**
- If you search for a contact by phone number to start a chat, that number is sent to our server to check whether it matches a registered user
- If you block or report another user, we store a record of that action

## How we use this information

- To provide core App functionality: sending messages, making calls, showing your contacts who's online
- To verify your identity at sign-up (via the OTP code sent to your phone number)
- To deliver push notifications for new messages and calls
- To enforce blocking and respond to reports of abuse
- To maintain the security and reliability of the service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Data storage and retention

- Messages, media, and account data are stored on our servers until you delete them or delete your account.
- Deleted messages ("delete for everyone") are removed from the database; media files are removed from storage.
- Stories automatically expire and are deleted 24 hours after posting.
- If you delete your account, your messages, media, and profile data are deleted, except where retained temporarily for legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes.

## Message and call security

- Messages and calls are transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS for messages, and WebRTC's mandatory encryption for call audio/video).
- **Note:** as of this version, Sainn does not implement end-to-end encryption. This means that, in principle, message content stored on our servers could be accessed by the operator of the service in the course of maintaining it. If you require end-to-end encrypted messaging, please be aware of this limitation.

## Third-party services

Sainn relies on the following third-party infrastructure to operate:
- **Push notifications:** delivered via Expo's push notification service.
- **Media storage:** photos, videos, and files you send may be stored using a cloud storage provider (Amazon S3 or compatible).
- **Call relay (TURN):** in some network conditions, call audio/video may be relayed through a TURN server to establish a connection; this server does not store call content.

## Your choices and rights

- You can control who sees your last-seen status and profile photo in Settings → Privacy.
- You can disable read receipts in Settings → Privacy (this also disables seeing others' read receipts).
- You can block or report any user from a chat's options menu.
- You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

## Children's privacy

Sainn is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country's law), and we do not knowingly collect information from children under that age.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
