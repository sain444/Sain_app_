Sainn ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated by Sainn (contact: tutiongpt@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information we collect

Account information: phone number, display name, profile photo, and username (if set).

Content you send: messages, photos, videos, files, and voice messages sent through the App; stories/updates you post (deleted automatically after 24 hours).

Usage and technical information: online/last-seen status, typing indicators, message delivery and read receipts (unless disabled in Settings), call metadata (who, when, duration, type — not call content), push notification device token, and basic technical/security logs such as IP address.

Information about other people: phone numbers you search to start a chat, and any block/report actions you take.

How we use this information

  • To provide core App functionality — messaging, calls, presence
  • To verify your identity at sign-up via OTP
  • To deliver push notifications
  • To enforce blocking and respond to abuse reports
  • To maintain security and reliability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data storage and retention

Data is stored until you delete it or your account. Deleted messages and media are removed from our systems. Stories expire automatically after 24 hours.

Message and call security

Messages and calls travel over encrypted connections (TLS / WebRTC's mandatory encryption). Sainn does not currently implement end-to-end encryption — message content stored on our servers could, in principle, be accessed by the service operator during normal maintenance.

Third-party services

Push notifications are delivered via Expo's push service. Media may be stored via a cloud storage provider (Amazon S3 or compatible). Call audio/video may be relayed through a TURN server in some network conditions, without storing call content.

Your choices and rights

  • Control last-seen and profile-photo visibility in Settings → Privacy
  • Disable read receipts (mutual — also hides others' receipts from you)
  • Block or report any user
  • Request account and data deletion by emailing us

Children's privacy

Sainn is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically; material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.
